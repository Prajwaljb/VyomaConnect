<p><b>Your Android phone and your Linux PC, working as one.</b> Set it up once. After that it just works: no fiddling, no developer switches left on. Open source, no account, no cloud.</p>

<p><b>Set up once, then forget about it.</b> Pair by scanning the code in the PC's window: nothing to type, nothing to compare. From then on the phone and PC find each other by themselves, over Wi-Fi, the USB cable the moment you plug it in, or Bluetooth when there's no Wi-Fi. If they ever can't connect, Vyoma says why in plain words, with the button that fixes it.</p>

<p><b>No developer switches left on.</b> No root, no Shizuku, and no need to keep USB debugging on. Reading the clipboard while Vyoma is closed is the one thing Android lets only a computer unlock: a one-time step from your PC, then USB debugging goes off again.</p>

<p><b>Your fingerprint guards your PC.</b> With the fingerprint lock on, unlocking the PC, opening its files, seeing its screen, using its camera and power buttons, the touchpad and the clipboard history all ask for your fingerprint first. Commands can ask every time, set from the PC so they can't be switched off from the phone.</p>

<p><b>Secure without thinking about it.</b> Encrypted, direct connections; each side remembers the other's certificate. Pairing codes work once, for ten minutes. The PC decides what each phone may do and logs what it did. Copies your password manager marks private never leave the phone.</p>

<p><b>What you get</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Files and whole folders both ways, resumable, around 70 MB/s over a cable; right-click Send to on the PC</li>
<li>One clipboard, with history</li>
<li>Calls and notifications on the PC, with replies and a Mute button</li>
<li>Your PC's screen on the phone, its stats, brightness, per-app volume, media, lock and power</li>
<li>The phone as touchpad and keyboard, presenter with a laser pointer, webcam and microphone, or a game controller with gyro aiming</li>
<li>Your own commands and rules, like locking the PC when you walk away</li>
<li>Widgets, Quick Settings tiles and lock-screen controls</li>
</ul>

<p>Vyoma's PC app runs on KDE Plasma and GNOME and installs with one line. The phone app also pairs with KDE Connect and GSConnect for the essentials.</p>

<p>Vyoma is an independent project, not affiliated with KDE.</p>
